2. Summary

I am an engineering student with a strong interest in research, focusing on the intersection of Plasma Engineering Technology, Embedded Hardware, and Technical Analytics. My primary academic focus is on learning the design and operation of plasma sources, high-voltage systems, and their applications in agriculture, medicine, and advanced materials. Alongside hands-on laboratory work, I have developed skills in hardware integration, C++, and IoT using Arduino and ESP32. My analytical background includes data processing, prediction, and machine learning modeling using Python (NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-Learn) and MATLAB. Driven by interdisciplinary innovation, I am committed to continuous learning and preparing for graduate research opportunities in applied physics and engineering.
